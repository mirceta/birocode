👮 The Kanban policeman — as one state diagram

Three nested levels, one directed graph. The agent (outer): the states the policeman conversation itself is in and what moves it. Pass running (middle): the steps of one pass, in order, with its two inner loops. Each card (inner, under step 4): the nine states a card can be in as the policeman sees it, what a pass finds to move it, and the one action it takes there. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan. Click a state to light its transitions; click it again, or the background, to clear.
