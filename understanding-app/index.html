Why this split

The harness owns everything that renders a conversation or talks to its API: one codebase for the chat UI, its own trust model, and a remote that works for any harness in the fleet with no living-room app at all. daljinski owns what only it can do on this PC: inject input, scroll, press keys, raise the right window — and give the phone one page with a Harness tab next to YouTube and the touchpad. The living-room branch feature/daljinski-text-send has a lot of uncommitted work (admin app, Haydar agent, playlists); the leg here is a separate small branch that does not touch it.
