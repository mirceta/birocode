What the store enforces, for everyone

  

    
RuleWhat happens

    
kind requiredprinciple · rule · fleet-fact · repo-note · goal-note · other

    
one-line reasonon every add, update and retire; it is kept in the version history

    
no secretstokens, keys, private keys, JWTs, connection strings with a password, URLs with credentials, password=… are refused. "It lives in the Credential Manager" is fine.

    
only you deletethe arch can only retire; you restore or delete

    
durableatomic write plus a .bak; a zeroed file (like the 10-16 crash) loads from the backup
