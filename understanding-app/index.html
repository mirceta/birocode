The hub pulls — because the fleet channel runs one way

A hub holds a credential for each peer; a peer holds none for its hub. So an agent's request is written on its own machine's store, and the hub pulls it (GET /api/arch/peer/requests) — every 30 s on the engine tick, every 10 s while the tab is open — keeping rows from repos in its fleet scope. A decision travels back (POST /api/arch/peer/requests/decision) behind the peer's "accept fleet sends" opt-in, the same gate every fleet write goes through.

Why not a queue inside the arch?

The arch conversation has no Operator-message queue by design: SendToArch starts a turn or says "mid-turn". The only queued path is the goal summary, drained one per engine tick. Reusing it keeps one rule for "something the harness posts into the Operator's conversation when the arch is free" — and keeps the Operator's own messages the only thing that resumes a stopped standing loop (a tagged actor never does).
