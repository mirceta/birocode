👮 The Kanban policeman — what it is, what it does, what it cannot do

openspec kanban-policeman-conversation · policeman-tool-surface · policeman-syncs-cards · policeman-observes-agents — the same data the product's "How it works" tab renders

A standing conversation with one job: keep the Kanban honest

The policeman is the arch agent in a reserved sibling conversation (@arch:policeman), driven by a forever recipe loop. Same skeleton as the arch — chat, tools, history, loops — but a smaller surface: it is offered its observe-verify-flag tools and the arch's acting tools are withheld at three fences (the tool list it is offered, the CLI's disallowed list, the server's call-time refusal).

👁
It reads

the board's verdict from the facts, every in-flight agent's last messages (on any machine), every repo's pull requests, git state.

✍️
It writes back three things

what each agent is doing (the card's Agent section) · a card moved forward to what GitHub proves · 🆘 Needs human with a reason.

🚫
It never

talks to an agent, moves a card by claim, creates / assigns / deletes, runs loops, or touches a manual card.

Every interval (default 5 min) one pass; at the context cap (default 400k tokens) the session is rolled over with a mechanical handover and the old session stays in History.

One pass, in order

    Step 2 is the sweep the Operator asked for: every card in Doing / Committed / PR open with an assignee gets its agent's last four messages read and its Agent section written. Step 3 is why a card no longer sits in Doing with an open PR: the PR is traced back to the card and the harness moves it.

    The two state machines a card lives in

    Kept apart on purpose. The Board check is judged from facts (git, GitHub, the deploy log). Progress is the column — and who may move it: an observation needs facts and only goes forward; a claim (an agent's line, the arch, you dragging) moves it anywhere, and the Board check then says whether the facts agree.

    The Agent section — what the policeman read

    Not a state machine: the policeman's latest reading of the agent's own words in its conversation. Always one of a fixed vocabulary, always with a one-sentence summary that stands alone, and always with who read it, when, and the policeman session.

      AGENT
      ❓ Asked a question
      asked whether the CSV should include voided invoices, 40 min ago, no answer yet
      — seen by the policeman, 4 min ago · session b7e2d1c0

      The three that mean "a human should look" — asked a question, blocked, errored — become a 🆘 flag once they have stood past the stale window.

      Where its provenance lives

      The rule on every card: a status names its source — the auto-verifier (git & PR facts), the policeman, the agent, or you — and when. Nothing on a card is a raw reason string; nothing is unattributed.
