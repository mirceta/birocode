What is deterministic (harness code) and what is prompt-driven (the model's judgement) is marked on every box and every arrow — the glyph on the box, the line style of the arrow, and the "colour: who decides" toggle. The rule of thumb: the harness owns timing, safety, facts, moves, fences and provenance; the model reads and judges inside a closed set of allowed writes.
