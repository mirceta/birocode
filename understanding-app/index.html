Why your loop stopped — and the three fixes

Your goal loop died at iteration 0, your goal looked erased after restart, and suggestion loops appeared armed on their own. All three are real, all three are fixed (OpenSpec change fix-loop-arm-freshness). Pick a tab.

The stale-reply trap

Every 10s the engine reads the agent's trailing chat message and runs the safety ladder over it: NEEDS_HUMAN: → deny-list words → sentinel. But at arm time that trailing message is just whatever you and the agent were last talking about — in your case our deploy conversation, full of the deny-listed word "deploy". The old engine judged that old message as if it were a reply to itself and escalated before sending anything. The fix: a reply older than the loop's arm time is history — the ladder only judges replies the loop actually caused.

The goal was never lost — the form just didn't read it back

Every parameter of every loop is written to %APPDATA%\ClaudeWeb\loops.json the moment you arm (goal text, composed work + verification prompts, cap, mode, phase, counters, stop reason). A restart reloads that file — your goal was sitting in it the whole time; the debug bundle you pasted proves it (loop.goal was fully populated).

The bug was one layer up: the dock popover's goal and cap fields were blank component state, never seeded from the store. Fix: opening the popover now reads this agent's persisted record (through the same operator-gated detail endpoint the prompt preview uses) and pre-fills goal, cap, and mode. Verified end-to-end: armed a goal loop, killed and restarted the harness, reopened the popover — goal and cap were back.

No loop runs unless you armed it

The rule is now absolute, and there was exactly one violator: a startup migration that converted the old suggestion-autopilot's armed-repo list (autopilot.json → ArmedRepoIds) into live suggestion loops when the harness booted. That is what you found armed after the restart and had to stop by hand.

Fix: the migration now just clears the legacy list and logs what it dropped — it arms nothing. The full arming truth table:
