Why Drive the feature / Finish and ship showed up on the agent dock with no framing, what they actually are, and the now-built change that makes the dock speak the same loop‑type vocabulary the new Autopilot nav introduced.

The “wtf” in one picture

The dock card whispered a word — Recipes — that the redesigned Autopilot nav never introduced you to. Two vocabularies, no bridge.

≠
So what are they? Drive the feature and Finish and ship are the two seeded recipes from the adopt‑autopilot‑loops change — named templates for the 🎯 goal‑based loop. They live in LoopRecipeStore.cs and are rendered by the console’s Goal‑based loop → Recipes subtab. The dock armed one of them but never said which kind of loop it was arming — that was the whole disconnect, fixed by align‑dock‑loop‑model (the Before → after tab shows the shipped popover).

Two loop types (and a third that’s only a plan)

Autopilot advances an idle agent in one of two ways. Click a card to expand it.

💡

Suggestion‑based loop

Watches an idle agent, then predicts one of your routine prompts.

  • Brain: a classifier picks from your editable prompt library.
  • Suggest‑only: it proposes, you press send.
  • Auto‑advance ON: a confident, non‑risky prompt is sent for you.
  • Risky / low‑confidence → it escalates instead.
  • Armed per agent in the console’s Control subtab.
Console: 💡 Suggestion‑based loop → Control · Prompt library · Live feed · History
🎯

Goal‑based loop

Resends one fixed goal prompt every turn until the job is done.

  • No brain: deterministic — the same prompt, resent each turn.
  • Stops on the sentinel (LOOP_DONE), NEEDS_HUMAN:, a deny‑list hit, the iteration cap, or a run error.
  • Armed from a recipe — a named template carrying prompt + sentinel + cap.
  • Seeded recipes: Drive the feature, Finish and ship.
  • One loop per agent.
Console: 🎯 Goal‑based loop → Agents · Recipes
🗂️

Queue‑based loop

Work a queue of tasks — doesn’t exist yet.

  • Only a plan card on the Overview today; no root tab, no code.
  • Stays off the dock — the dock is for actions, not plans.
Console: Overview → three‑mode plan

    The change — align‑dock‑loop‑model

    Three moves, plus the judgment calls. Built and verified (17/17 Playwright checks on an isolated harness) as an OpenSpec change on feat/autopilot‑tab‑hierarchy.

    1. 1

      Popover mirrors the console nav

      Two captioned sections — 💡 Suggestion‑based and 🎯 Goal‑based — each with a one‑line what‑it‑does. The suggestion section adds a per‑agent Arm/Disarm (same as Control); the goal section keeps the recipe picker + cap + Arm, unchanged.

      client/src/components/dashboard/DockLoopControl.jsx
    2. 2

      Typed badge

      🎯 2/10 while a goal loop runs (terminal states as today); a small 💡 marker when the agent is suggestion‑armed. The glance now tells you the kind.

      DockLoopControl.jsx (badge)
    3. 3

      Honest badge data

      Extend the read‑only, non‑gated GET /api/autopilot/loops projection with each repo’s suggestion‑armed flag — so the 💡 marker is truthful even when the operator gate is closed. Arming still goes through the gated endpoints (403 → the existing “gate is closed” hint).

      AutopilotController · loops projection
