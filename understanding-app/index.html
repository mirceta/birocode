Restructuring the Autopilot Console 10 flat tabs → a 5-root hierarchy grouped by which loop owns them

The console grew flat: ten sibling tabs with no visible grouping. But those tabs aren't siblings — they belong to different loop types. This app shows the fix, now shipped (openspec restructure-autopilot-tabs): color every tab by its owner, then fold the flat strip into a hierarchy that mirrors reality.

2 · Three loop types — the real organizing axis

Autopilot isn't one thing. It's up to three independent loops, each with its own tabs. The restructure groups by this axis instead of ignoring it.

LIVE

💡 Suggestion loop

Watches idle armed agents; picks one of your routine prompts (with a confidence score) or escalates. Its whole vocabulary is the prompt library.
Owns 6 of the 10 tabs:
  • Agents (control)
  • Routine prompts
  • Intercepted
  • Suggestion history
  • + most of Audit
LIVE

🎯 Goal loop

Resends a fixed prompt to drive an agent toward a goal — iteration counter, progress, stop-reason. A different mechanism from suggestions.
Owns 1 tab with two unrelated halves:
  • Agents (arm / status / stop-reason)
  • Recipes (reusable goal templates)
NOT BUILT

📋 Queue loop

Doesn't exist yet. Its card lives on the Overview; when built it arrives as its own root tab beside the other two — not shoehorned in early.
Owns 0 tabs today.
  • (future) queue view
Key insight: Audit is the one record that spans both live loops — the backend writes both auto-advance sends and goal-loop resends (outcome="loop") into the same AutopilotAuditLog. That's why it becomes a root tab, not a child of either loop.

3 · Where every old tab goes

A pure re-home. No views are merged or redesigned — each of the 10 flat tabs moves to a new address (some renamed for clarity).

Overview stays as-is (front page, gate-exempt). The goal-loop's single tab splits into its two natural halves.

4 · The shipped hierarchy

Two-level nav: a root row grouped by loop, then a subtab row (with per-root subtab memory). Click a ▸ root to expand/collapse. The same component renders in the routed tab and the dashboard dock, so the shape is identical everywhere.

5 · Judgment calls baked in

Surfaced, not asked — decisions baked into the shipped change.

6 · The one question — resolved

The single open choice was where Audit lives. Decision: root tab.

Audit is a root tab

It's the durable record of what autopilot sent while you were away — spanning both live loops, so it belongs to neither. It's the thing you'd check after leaving autopilot unattended: a first-class destination, not documentation. Each entry now carries a kind badge — sent (suggestion auto-advance) vs loop (goal-loop resend) — read straight from the backend's recorded outcome.

Also baked in: pure restructure (no feature merges — Live feed + History stay separate for now), the gate keeps fencing everything except Overview (the nav itself always stays visible), and it ships as the OpenSpec change restructure-autopilot-tabs, stacked on the loops-adoption branch (the goal-loop tab it reorganizes isn't on main yet).
