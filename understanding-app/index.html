Your ask, as I understood it, before I build it: on the projector the agent dock is too small to read — its top part (lanes, app chips, understanding / goal rows, engine, git) eats the height and the chat gets what is left. You want one new button on the phone that does both at once — chat maximized, and if an app is pushed, the app beside it, chat 30 % / app 70 %.
