Goal conversations: the arch on a timer

One sentence: a goal conversation is the arch agent re-prompted every N minutes; on each turn it checks its agents itself; the agents never call anyone. Nothing else moves on its own. Press play.

Each poll is the same turn: the harness re-sends the goal (plus anything you queued); the arch calls list_agents (who is running?), read_transcript (what did a finished one say?), list_tasks (the board), then acts: sends a task, follows up, moves a card — or says "nothing changed yet" and ends the turn. That last line is the accepted cost.

An agent finishing changes nothing by itself. Its reply sits in its own conversation until the next poll reads it. Try: press play, watch prg finish, and see the goal conversation only notice at the next poll.

How it ends

  1. The arch judges the goal finished and ends a reply with LOOP_DONE.
  2. The harness asks it once to verify; GOAL_VERIFIED ends the loop. (Or: your Stop, the cap, an error. NEEDS_HUMAN: … keeps it busy with the question shown; your reply is queued for the next poll.)
  3. The conversation releases its agents, becomes free, and one summary lands in your chat.
